Curtain · shapes

Press Play or click the screen. Each run: a beat of flat black while the page loads, then it leaves. Slow ×4 shows the shape.

1 · Last pixel — the black breaks into square pixels that wink out in a loose scatter from the top, and one pixel stays a moment longer, by the word "pixel" in your sentence, before it goes too. The headline's claim, said once, without a word. My pick.

2 · Page curve — the black lifts with its corners first and its middle hanging lowest, the same curve the page makes over the black footer, so the site opens and ends on one shape.

3 · Liquid — the edge is a slow, heavy wave, like ink being drawn up; it wobbles more while moving fast and flattens as it leaves.

4 · Watercolour edge — a rough, bleeding brush edge with a paler wash just under it, after your own paintings.

5 · Blinds — ten horizontal slats close up one after another from the top, like a blind being drawn.

6 · Paper fold — the black folds up like a sheet of paper in an accordion, each fold a shade lighter or darker.

7 · Aperture — a circle opens from your sentence and grows until the black is gone, like a camera iris.

8 · Doors — the black parts in the middle and the two halves slide away left and right.

On the site any of these would still wait for the page to load, skip on a key or click, and simply fade under reduced motion.
