Sound, round 9 · the wall's tick

A quieter tick

Why the detent croaked on the site: its body is a tone that falls in pitch (1300 → 700 Hz in 25ms). In round 8 it played softly through a room echo and a limiter, which blurred the fall into a click. On the site it plays dry and twice as loud, so you hear the fall as a "ribbit".

So this round plays every sound the way the site does: dry, straight out, at the site's level. No volume slider and no echo, so what you hear here is what the site will play. None of them slide in pitch, and none are a musical note. Each is one small sound as a piece crosses the middle of the screen, and silence when you stop. "Live now" is the current detent, for comparison.

A · Detent, no croak
The same detent with the falling tone taken out: just its crisp top and a short wooden body that holds one pitch.
B · Watch crown
The finest click there is, like winding a mechanical watch. Very high, very short. At speed it becomes a soft ratchet.
C · Film advance
A film camera's lever moving one frame: two tiny clicks, the second fainter. One frame per picture.
D · Pencil
A pencil tapped once on paper, with a little grain. Her drawings' sound, and the driest of the set.
E · Felt
A soft, low tap, like a felt piano hammer on a muted string. More felt than heard; the warmest.
F · Bead
A wooden bead on an abacus meeting the one next to it. Woody and round, lighter than a knock.
G · Closed hat
Techno's quietest drum, a closed hi-hat, very short and turned well down. Scroll fast and it plays a rhythm. Her techno side, without any music.

Press Sound off so it reads Sound on, pick one in the bar, and scroll down slowly and then fast. The Play buttons above give one tick each.

My pick, if any: D · Pencil or B · Watch crown. They're the smallest of the set, and the wall's sound is heard more than any other on the site, so smaller wears better. A is the safe one if you liked the detent's idea and only the croak was wrong.

If none of these still feels right after a minute of scrolling, leaving the wall silent is a good answer too.
