Sound, round 4

A summer in the countryside

Round 4, from the site's Totoro feel: an old house in the Japanese countryside in summer. A wind bell under the eaves, a koto, a bamboo fountain in the garden, rain on leaves; for Moss, a heavy, soft creature walking through a forest floor. Notes use the Japanese yo scale (five notes, bright and open). Every sound is made here, in the browser: no film music or film sound effects, nothing copied. Turn sound on above, pick a variant, then try it.

01 · Playground wall

Walking the wall

A · Wind bell (furin) — the small glass bell under a summer house's eaves. A clear "ting" each time a painting passes the middle while you drag or press an arrow; now and then a second, fainter ting, as if the breeze caught it. Never on scroll. My pick: a gallery on a summer porch.

B · Koto — a plucked koto string per painting, on the yo scale, so a drag plays a short phrase that climbs one way and falls the other.

C · Bamboo fountain (shishi-odoshi) — water trickles quietly while the wall moves; when it comes to rest, the bamboo tips with one hollow "tock". One sound per move, the calmest of the four.

D · Rain on leaves — a few soft drops patter as you drag, faster the faster you go, and stop when you stop.

02 · Preloader

Moss’s steps

Same catch as last time: the curtain runs on a fresh open or refresh, when sound is always off, so these need the curtain to replay after a press (say, turning the speaker on at the top of the home page) to be heard. The steps here land on a steady beat; on the site they'd be matched to his paws in the clip.

I ship, I nudge, I craft, from systems and strategy to the last stubborn pixel.

A · Forest floor — a deep, soft thump per step with a faint rustle of leaves and grass under the paw. Heavier and slower while he pushes. My pick: big, soft, outdoors.

B · Acorn — the same steps, and as the curtain leaves, an acorn drops with a tiny wooden "tok" and one little bounce.

C · Tatami — indoors: muffled thumps on a straw mat, with a soft brush of the mat under each paw. The quietest.

D · Steps and a bell — the forest steps, and one wind bell "ting" as the curtain leaves and the page arrives. Ties the curtain to the playground's bell.
