Sound, round 7

One small sound

No music. One short sound, under a second or two, when you stop at a painting in the museum — the same size as the site's other sounds. Each mixes the two sides you named: a warm, Ghibli-like note (music box, glass, a soft pluck on the bright yo scale) shaped the way techno shapes sound (a filter sweep, an echo, a sub under it, a little digital grit). Each painting has its own note, so stopping at a few in turn sounds like a phrase, never a track. Nothing plays while you drag.

Press Sound off once so it reads Sound on (one soft note confirms it). Pick a variant, then rest the pointer on a painting for a moment. Tab moves between paintings too.

Step into a painting

A · Soft pluck — a warm note whose brightness closes fast, the way a techno synth plucks, with two soft echoes trailing into the hall. Round, a little electronic, never sharp. My pick: warm first, techno in the shape.

B · Music box, echoed — one music-box note, then an echo that repeats and darkens, like a dub delay. The most Ghibli of the four, with the techno in its tail.

C · Bell and sub — a clear glass ting with a very soft low thump under it, felt more than heard, like a kick from a distant club.

D · Pixel chime — two quick notes, slightly grainy, like a gentle game sound. Answers the site's "last stubborn pixel" line; the most playful.
