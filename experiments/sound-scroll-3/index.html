Sound, round 10 · the wall

Beyond the tick

No clicks this round. Every option follows how you scroll, not each picture: silent when the page is still, rising as you move, and fading out when you stop. Everything plays the way the site would play it (no echo, the site's level), so what you hear is what you'd get.

Like the hum, but calmer

Was · Hum
Round 8's "Hum that opens", for comparison. It felt intense because of its buzzy tone, the deep bass and a filter that opens wide and whistles.
A · Low hum, softened
The same chord at the same depth, with the buzz, the whistle and most of the brightness taken out. Moving the page warms it a little instead of opening it wide.
B · Warm pad
A soft synth chord, moved up out of the bass, that swells in slowly as you scroll and settles away when you stop. A synth, but a gentle one.
C · Tremolo keys
An electric-piano chord held with a slow wobble, like late-night lo-fi. Scrolling faster makes the wobble a little quicker.

Something different

D · Vinyl
The quiet crackle of a record under the needle, crackling more the faster you scroll. It echoes the record player on About, and it's warm without being music.
E · Shaker
A soft seed shaker that moves with your hand: slow scroll, a few grains; a fast flick, a quick shake. Percussion, so it nods to techno, but there's no beat.
F · Lift and land
Nothing while you scroll. There's a breath of air as you start and two low, soft piano notes as you come to rest, as if the wall has settled. It's the quietest overall, because it's only heard twice per scroll.

Press Sound off so it reads Sound on, pick one in the bar, and scroll down slowly, then fast, then stop. The Play buttons above act out one short scroll for you.

My pick, if any: D · Vinyl. It's a texture rather than a note, so it never tires the way a chord can, and it ties the playground to the record player. B · Warm pad is the nearest to the hum you liked. F is the one to choose if you want sound there but barely.
