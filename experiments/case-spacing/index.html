Case study spacing, and quieter tags

Measured at 1440px on the PayPal Debit Card case. The page has three left edges (strip 40px, rail 64px, content 291px), the snippet runs 150 characters wide, the square cover sits in a 16:7 lilac letterbox so 60% of it is empty hue, and every block below the cover floats 96px from the next, so numbers, the "Business" note and the snapshot read as three loose parts instead of one. Pick a tag colour below and it applies to every mock.
