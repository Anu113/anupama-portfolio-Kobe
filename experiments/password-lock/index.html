Locked cases, before the click

Hover a locked card: its picture frosts over, a small lock says “Password protected”, and the card still clicks through to the gate you already have, where they type the passcode or email you. At rest a locked card already shows the small lock by its company name, so nobody has to hover to find out. Try the keyboard too: Tab onto a card and it frosts the same way.
