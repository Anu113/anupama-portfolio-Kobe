Curtain colours

What colour is the curtain?

Each frame is the preloader halfway: the hero sentence on the page's ground, Moss pushing the curtain off to the right. Only the curtain's colour changes. Click a frame to play the push. Grain adds the paper texture from your screenshot; Dark mode shows the page's dark ground (the ink curtain re-inks light there, the others stay as they are).

The curtain carries no words, so contrast isn't a rule here, but Moss's edge against it is: very dark and very light curtains keep him easiest to read. The second dot under each name is a type colour that would go with it if the curtain ever carried words, like your screenshot's salmon; today it carries none, and the sentence stays in ink.
