The archive, strung

The hairlines between the /work archive rows are the harp. Move the pointer up and down across them. Turn Sound on to hear each line's note, the same harp as today.

A · Catch: the line catches the pointer as it crosses, bends with it a little (up to 14px), then slips off and rings. The note plays as it lets go.
B · Strum: nothing catches; crossing a line sets it ringing, harder the faster you cross. The note plays on the cross.
C · Deep pull: as A, but the line holds on longer (up to 28px) and rings slower and longer, like a bass string.

Keyboard: Tab through the rows and each row's top line gives a small ring. Under reduced motion the lines stay straight; the notes still play.
