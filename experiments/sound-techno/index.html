Sound, round 6

Techno, with a soft heart

The playground as a museum that's also a little bit of a club: a techno pulse underneath, and over it warm, Ghibli-like music — piano, music box, soft pads, a bright five-note scale — that opens up when you stop at a painting. Each painting has its own short melody. The chords follow a progression loved in Japanese pop (IV–V–iii–vi). All composed and synthesised here; no film music copied.

Press Sound off once so it reads Sound on (one soft note confirms it). Pick a variant, move the pointer onto the wall, and rest on a painting for a beat. Tab moves between paintings too.

Playground

Step into the music

A · The next room — on the wall, a techno beat plays as if through a museum wall: muffled kick, rolling bass. Rest on a painting and the door opens: the filter sweeps open, warm chords swell in, and the painting's own piano melody plays over the beat. Move on and the door closes again. My pick: the museum and the club in one, and the painting is what opens it.

B · Build the track — the wall starts with only a kick. Each painting you look at adds its part — hats, bassline, pads, an arp, piano, a clap, a music box — and the parts stay while you're on the wall, so looking around builds the whole track. Leave and it fades.

C · Drag the arp — a quiet beat; dragging the wall plays a fast techno arpeggio on the current chord, louder the faster you drag. Stop on a painting and the arp settles into its melody over a pad.

D · 3am lo-fi — slower and softer: a dusty beat, Rhodes-like chords and a bit of record crackle; each painting adds its melody on a music box. The gentlest; least techno.
