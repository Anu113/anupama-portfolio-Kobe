Okta · Identity & Access Management · Okta AI

Agentic identity security

Okta AI agents look for identity risk all day. The admin makes the calls that need a person.

Role
Staff Product Designer · Sep 2024 – Mar 2025
Team
Okta IAM
Tools

01 · The challenge

Five tabs to answer one question

Okta admins jumped between the Admin Console, System Log, Identity Governance, Workflows and their own scripts to work out whether an alert mattered and who it touched.

Slower answers, context lost between tabs, and more risk on the calls that can lock a whole company out.

Project goal

Design one continuous flow where Okta AI agents investigate identity risk, carry out the tasks they're allowed to, and hand the admin a shared finding when a person has to decide.

02 · Human + AI collaboration

Agents do the legwork. People make the calls.

Agents watch the org, investigate what looks wrong and act inside the permissions an admin gave them. When judgement is needed they write up a finding the admin can check, change and approve before anything is fixed.

  1. ◆ AgentWatchSign-ins, roles, policies, all day
  2. ◆ AgentInvestigateLinks users, groups, rules and logs
  3. ◆ AgentWrite a findingWhat, why, what to do
  4. ● AdminCheck the workEvidence, against org policy
  5. ● AdminDecideSuggested fix, or their own
  6. ◆ AgentVerifyReports if the fix held

↺ back to Watch

03 · AI investigation

The agent finds the pattern

It reads identity relationships, policy rules and sign-in signals across the org and raises only what needs a person.

Review moves the admin from the agent's investigation into their own review. The finding, its signals and its context come along, so nothing has to be looked up again.

04 · Policy agent summary of findings

What it found, why it matters, what to do

The Policy agent states the gap, why it's risky, and the safest next step, in three short parts.

05 · Human audit

Check the agent's work

The admin sees the relationships and checks behind the suggestion, and confirms it fits their org's policy before going on. The one check only a person can make is left for them.

06 · Shared decision making

From analysis to action

Once reviewed, the fix is framed as a shared decision: the agent suggests the best next step and estimates its impact, and nothing changes until the admin picks.

They can take the suggestion or change it for their own context, policy or appetite for risk.

07 · Verification loop

Did the fix hold?

After the fix, the agent keeps watching and reports whether it did what it was meant to.

09 · Outcome

One place from alert to fix

Shipped to early-access customers in the Admin Console, setting one pattern that joined investigation, decision support and the fix in one flow.

22 → 6 minfrom a high-risk alert to the admin’s decision
68%of agent findings approved with the suggested fix, unchanged
10 of 12admins in testing went from review to approve without help
  • 94% of applied fixes still held when the agent re-checked them 30 days later, with no admins locked out
  • More confidence in fix choices, and decisions admins can explain
  • A reusable pattern for agent tasks across Okta
