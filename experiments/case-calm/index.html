01 The situation

75% of settings traffic stopped on the first page, and every new rule had nowhere to go

A list of controls in legal language, with no structure underneath. Customer service heard the same privacy questions again and again. Two readers had to be satisfied by the same screens.

A PayPal customer

One tap

What is PayPal doing with my data, and how do I change it?

  • Find any setting from the privacy page
  • See what it's set to, and what it does
  • Get a copy, or delete part, and keep the account

GDPR and CCPA

Every screen

Is everything disclosed, and is what must be kept, kept?

  • Say what's collected, why, and whether it's shared
  • Honour requests to see or delete data
  • Name what PayPal must retain, not leave it silent

02 The hard call

The first structure tested well and cost a tap. I kept the groups and dropped the extra layer.

The first version came straight from the card sorts: four tiles, each opening a sheet of settings. In rapid research the groups worked. The sheets didn't, and some of the words failed.

What rapid research found, and what changed

SeverityFoundChanged
StructureThe sheets cost one more tap before any setting, on every task.Sheets dropped: one page, three headings. 2 → 1 taps to any setting.
CriticalPeople couldn't tell deals from ads.Interest-based marketing became "Deals & ads" under Permissions and shared data, with "Learn how it works".
MediumTo change marketing email, people looked under Notifications, not Data and Privacy.Emails and notifications got their own link at the bottom, where people looked.
MediumPeople read "Correct your data" as something other than updating their details."Correct your data" became "Update your data".

What we missed

The card sorts settled where every setting goes, not what it's called. Two of the four findings were words, and we tested the labels after the structure.

03 How the work got made

Four structures, three prototypes, and only one cleared the 70% bar

Four closed card sorts tested the current structure against three alternatives, on 20 tasks. Before any screen, I worked out what each layer of the settings had to hold, and what it might look like.
