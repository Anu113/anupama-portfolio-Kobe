Sound, round 3

Two more sounds

Round 2's noise sounds are gone (knock, wire, slide, paper throw). This round the wall gets soft notes, all on one five-note scale so a fast drag sounds like a little tune instead of a clatter, and Moss gets footsteps: heavy, round and soft, like a big plush creature. Synthesised in the browser, no files, only with the speaker on. Turn sound on above, pick a variant, then try it.

01 · Playground wall

Walking the wall

A · Water drop — a soft, round "plip" each time a piece passes the middle of the screen while you drag or press an arrow: a drop of water landing in the jar between brush strokes. Notes climb as you pull one way and fall the other. My pick: it belongs to watercolours.

B · Kalimba — the same moments, as a thumb-piano note: warm, wooden, a little ring after it.

C · Music box — a tiny comb "pling" per piece, as if dragging the wall turns the handle of a music box. Faster drag, faster tune.

D · Glass on hover — silent while you drag; one soft glass note when you rest on a piece and its caption shows, a different note per piece.

02 · Preloader

Moss’s steps

Same catch as last time: the curtain runs on a fresh open or refresh, when sound is always off, so these need the curtain to replay after a press (say, turning the speaker on at the top of the home page) to be heard. The steps here land on a steady beat; on the site they'd be matched to his paws in the clip.

I ship, I nudge, I craft, from systems and strategy to the last stubborn pixel.

A · Plush thumps — a deep, soft "pmf" per step, left and right a touch apart, like a big stuffed animal walking on a wooden floor. Slower and heavier while he pushes. My pick.

B · Tummy bounce — the same thump with a tiny round "boing" on top, as if his belly bounces each step. The cutest, and the busiest.

C · Soft squeak — the thump with a faint toy squeak, like rubber paw pads. Plays only every other step so it doesn't nag.

D · Heave and stroll — light quick pats as he strolls in and out, and slow heavy thumps with a small "hnn" of effort while he pushes. Tells the story most, a bit more sound overall.
