Sound, round 5

A museum of moments

Not a wall you walk past: a quiet museum where each painting holds a moment, and sound is how you step into it. Nothing plays while you move; sound comes when you stop and look (rest the pointer on a piece, or focus one with Tab). Everything sits in one soft room echo, so it feels like a hall, not a speaker. All made here in the browser: no recordings, nothing from any film.

Press Sound off once so it reads Sound on: you will hear one soft note to confirm. Then pick a variant and rest the pointer on a painting for a second or two; the line under the wall says what you are hearing. Tab moves between paintings too.

Playground

Step into a painting

A · Living paintings — each painting carries the sound of its own moment, faint, as if you'd leaned close: morning birds on the porch, the breeze and a cup set down by the reading window, glass bangles for the three sisters, an earring's tiny bells, an old clock for the couple, rain for the girl in grey, near silence for "Quiet, for now", a small spark for the young witch. It fades in when you stop and out when you move on. My pick: it's the museum of experience, and it tells a hiring manager these are moments she painted, not files.

B · The hall answers — no scenes. Rest on a painting and a single low, soft note blooms and rings out in the hall's echo, each painting its own note on the Japanese yo scale, so looking at a few in turn makes a slow chord. The most "gallery", the least literal.

C · Times of day — the room changes as you travel along: morning birds at the start, summer cicadas by afternoon, rain in the evening, crickets at night, crossfading as you drag. One quiet bed that only plays while the pointer is over the wall.

D · The room leans in — a faint museum air under the wall; rest on a painting and the room hushes around it and one warm tone rises, as if the space is listening with you. Leave and the air comes back.
