A way to replay the phone

The case-study phones play their flow once when they come on screen, or the visitor taps through them. Each box below is the real device box with a stand-in four-screen flow; it plays once when you scroll to it, then stops on “Card ordered”. Tap the phone’s button to step through yourself. Five places to put the replay.

A · Corner

A small round button in the box’s top-right, always there, grey until hovered. Easy to find, but it sits on the box at rest even when there’s nothing to replay.

Card sign-up

B · In the pill

The label pill becomes the button: “Card sign-up” with the replay mark after it. Nothing new on the box; the pill lifts to white on hover.

C · Beside the pill

The label stays a label; a round replay sits next to it, the same glass, like a quiet version of the site’s pill pair.

Card sign-up

D · When it’s done

Nothing at rest. Once the flow reaches its end, or the visitor has tapped away from the start, the label slides out and “Replay” slides in, in the same spot. Shows only when there’s something to replay. My pick.

E · On the glass

A small dark button over the phone’s screen, top-right, on hover only (always on touch). Closest to the work, but it covers the product’s own UI.
