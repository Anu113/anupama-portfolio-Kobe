Real recordings

Every sound before this round was made in the browser out of plain tones and noise. These are real recordings, trimmed to a moment, the treble softened, and played the way the site would play them (no echo, the site's level). They come from the University of Iowa's instrument recordings (free for any use) and a public-domain recording of a pencil on paper.

The detent the site plays today, for comparison.
A · Pencil
One short pencil stroke on paper as each piece crosses the middle of the screen. No two in a row are the same stroke.
B · Marimba
A soft yarn-mallet marimba note as pieces pass, at most about three a second, wandering through a five-note scale.
C · Piano settle
Silent while you scroll. When you stop, a real piano, played very softly, gives one warm chord (D, A, D, F♯).
D · Pencil + settle
The pencil while you move, the piano when you land.

Press Sound off so it reads Sound on, pick one, and scroll down slowly, then fast, then stop. The Play buttons act out one short scroll.

My pick: C · Piano settle. It's the softest and the rarest, and a real piano played quietly is about as close to universally pleasant as a sound gets. D is the one if you want the wall to answer your hand as well.
