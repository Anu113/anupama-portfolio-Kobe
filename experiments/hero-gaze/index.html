Why she misses the cursor today

The cursor picks a moment in her clip, not a direction. Moving right she looks right, turns away, looks left, then down; moving down turns her too. And on the way she blinks and winks, because those are in the clip.

What changes

The direction from her face to the cursor picks where she looks: left over the headline, at you near her, right, down at Moss, down at the laptop. Near poses play through her own frames; far ones are a blink and a look.
