Sound, round 8 · scroll

The wall, scrolled

A rough copy of the playground: scroll and the columns of her work rise past, each at its own pace. Each variant makes sound only from the scroll's movement — silent when the page is still, louder and quicker the faster you go — and stops the moment you stop.

Press Sound off so it reads Sound on (a soft note confirms), pick a variant in the bar, and scroll down. Try slow, then a fast flick. The variant buttons stay at the top.

Speed

A · Detent — a soft, tight click each time a piece crosses the middle of the screen, like the notched wheel of an iPhone date picker or a watch crown. Slow scroll: separate clicks you can count. Fast flick: they run into a soft purr. Warm wooden body, clean electronic edge. My pick, if any: it's the one people already know from their phones, and it says "you moved one piece" and nothing more.

B · Pass-by notes — the same moments, but each crossing is a soft music-box note, pitched by where the piece sits on the wall, so scrolling plays a run on the bright yo scale. Prettiest; closest to "music", so it may tire fastest.

C · Hum that opens — one low, soft chord that's silent at rest; scrolling turns it up and opens its filter like a DJ's knob, faster = brighter. The techno one.

D · Breeze chord — air through tall grass, tuned to a soft chord rather than plain noise, rising and falling with the scroll's speed.

Honest take: scroll is the one thing every visitor does constantly, so a sound on it is heard far more than any other on the site. If none of these still feels right after a minute of scrolling, the right call is to leave the playground silent.
